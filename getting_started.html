1. Get a club

You start on the waiting list. There are two ways to get a club.

Club auction

Admin invites you when a club is up for auction. Before you can bid:

  • Set your owner tag, timezone, and match availability on Owner Details. The tag locks once you are invited.
  • On Club Database, mark one club you want and one backup. Both are required before a bid.

You bid from the starting budget shown on Owner Details. You can lead one club at a time. The highest bidder wins. The bid pays for the club and the stadium. Whatever is left becomes your club balance. The full menus then open.

Mid-season handpick

If a club is empty during the season, admin can hand it to you. You do not bid. You keep that club’s cash, the old squad is released, and you build a new one with Handpick.

2. Check the game before you take a player

Do this every time. Open eFootball and make sure the player is still in the game. GPSL can still list someone who has been removed. If they are not in your game, do not tag them, bid, or sign them. The same note sits on Handpick, the player database, the transfer market, drafts, and auctions.

3. Handpick a squad

Handpick is only while your club is in intake. A Handpick chip shows in the nav, and the Dashboard has a banner.

  1. In GPDB, tick free agents in the Handpick column. A tick is not a signing.
  2. Open Handpick Squad. The checklist must be green: squad size, home-grown, under-21, a goalkeeper, the star cap, and cash.
  3. Choose the planned One of our Own, or a Fan Favourite if your nation has no star players.
  4. Press Submit for approval.

When admin approves, those players are signed at market value plus the income tax shown on the handpick page. The money leaves the club at that moment. Tags you left off the approved list are not signed.

4. Handpick a manager

  1. Open MGDB and tick one manager in the Handpick column.
  2. Check them on Handpick Squad. The fee is their market value.
  3. Submit with the squad. The manager is assigned when the list is approved, and the fee is charged then.

Outside handpick, managers are bought on the Manager Market or in a manager draft when that auction is switched on.

5. Squad requirements

Squad is the live checklist. From August a club needs all of these:

  • At least 24 players, and no more than 28.
  • At least 8 home-grown players. Home-grown means their nation is your club’s nation.
  • At least 5 players aged 21 or under.
  • A goalkeeper.
  • Star cap, from rating 79 upward. Super League 3. Championship 2. One of our Own does not count toward the cap.

If you are short

Sign the missing type before August locks. Home-grown from your nation, under-21s at age 21 or younger, a goalkeeper if you have none.

If August locks and you are still short, or over the star cap, the league fills the gap for you. That means a fine, and often an emergency loan rated 72 or lower. At 28 players, someone is released to make room. One of our Own is protected.

In your first season at a club you have 3 slots. Each slot is one release or one transfer listing. Squad shows how many are left.

6. Money, and the easy ways to lose it

Open Finances before you submit a handpick or place a bid. The balance there is what you can spend.

  • A handpick approval spends market value plus tax immediately. Add the fees on the handpick page before you submit.
  • A winning auction bid is collected. Do not bid money you need for the squad.
  • Handpick will not submit if it would push you through the overdraft floor shown on that page.
  • Fines come off the balance when they are applied. They are not a bill you pay later.
  • Signing a 29th player releases someone else. If a foreign sale is available it is used. Otherwise the club is fined and that player is locked until next season.
  • Wages, the stadium, and tax sit on Finances. Season accounts is the full workbook.

7. GPSL months

A GPSL month is about one real-world week. The season runs June, July, August, and on through May, then playoffs. The month changes on Thursday at 7pm UK.

A league match can be played in its month and the two months after. An August match is finished when October locks.

A cup tie can be played in its round month and the one month after. May cups have to be played in May. If a cup’s round month has already locked and the tie is still unplayed, play that cup before other fixtures.

The dates for this season are on Season Calendar.

8. Set when you can play

On Owner Details, set your timezone and the hours you can play. Kick-off times offered to you are built from that grid, matched to the other owner’s hours.

Book a holiday there too, before the month opens. A holiday does not add an extra month onto a cup. It can let you arrange a league match early. If the match’s own month is covered by the holiday and it is still unplayed when the window ends, your club loses it.

9. Arrange a match

  1. Open the match from Fixtures or My club fixtures.
  2. Home proposes a kick-off. For a league match, do that in the month before it is due. For a cup, do it in the round month. Away can open a league match after that earlier month locks, and a cup after the round month locks, if home still has not proposed.
  3. The other club has 48 hours to accept or send a different time. Those 48 hours can run into the next GPSL month.
  4. You can agree in Discord, then both clubs must press We agreed on Discord on the schedule page. The reply clock keeps running until that is locked.
  5. Save a valid squad on Match Day. Check in on the schedule page from 10 minutes before kick-off until 10 minutes after.

10. Use My Calendar

My Calendar is the list of what is due for your club: proposals, replies, kick-offs, and the locks that close unplayed matches. Open it whenever you log in. A deadline you only see on Discord is easy to miss. The calendar is the one that matches the locks.

11. Avoid fines and forfeits

  • Propose on time. A missed proposal is a strike and ₿500,000. Each later lock with still no proposal is another strike.
  • Reply within 48 hours. A missed reply is the same strike. Still silent at the next lock is another. The proposal stays open.
  • A league proposal in the last 48 hours before the match month opens is a ₿1,000,000 late fee. Cups do not have that fee.
  • Check in. A miss is a strike and ₿500,000. If both clubs miss, you pick a new time.
  • Three strikes on the same match is ₿1,000,000 and a 3–0 defeat.
  • Every 5th strike in the season (5, 10, 15…) adds another ₿1,000,000.
  • Leave a match unplayed until its window locks and it is awarded. More strikes on that match loses. Still level, and a missed check-in or a kick-off left sitting after the 48-hour reply decides it. Still level after that, and the match is void. Nobody gets the win.
