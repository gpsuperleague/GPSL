How the projection works
  • Club expectation comes from prestige and tier (medium and low clubs get a lift for a strong manager). Finishing below it is a slight (1–2 places), bad (3–5) or abysmal (6+) miss.
  • Cups: reaching a club cup target (set by division and tier) cancels a slight league miss — no board fine, no transfer request. Bad and abysmal misses can't be rescued by cups. A manager's rule can also carry a cup target: if he misses his league target by 2 places or fewer (and isn't relegated), reaching that cup stage counts as a target hit. "Still in" means the club can still reach the stage; "out" means it has been knocked out before it.
  • Missing expectation means the board fines the owner 25% of their personal wealth (GPSL Building Society), and one player asks to be listed: big clubs lose a top-4 player (a lower-rated one for a slight miss), medium clubs a 74–78 player aged 22+ (68–73 for a slight miss), low clubs a player rated 72 or below. The player is picked at random from the names shown and listed at market value until sold.
  • Managers sign 2-season deals. After season 1, the board sacks the manager if the club has a bad or abysmal miss (3+ places below expected, or relegated when not expected to be). Owners can still sack in June/July or January for half market value. At the end of the deal: no personal target hit in either season → he refuses a new deal and leaves; a target hit but the club missed expectation in both seasons → he is sacked; otherwise he is open to renew for 2 more seasons (June/July). Leaving or sacked managers pay the club their market value and can't be re-signed for 2 seasons.
  • Finances: projected close = current balance + remaining gates, prize money, TV, subsidies, wages, manager salary, taxes and stadium maintenance. Below zero adds end-of-season debt interest. At or below −₿100m the club pays a ₿50m FFP fine, its most valuable players are sold at market value until the balance is back above the line, and it can't buy in the next transfer window.
  • Everything here is a projection — results, sales and spending between now and the end of the season will change it.
