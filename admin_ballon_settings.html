Stat weights by playstyle

Average rating, goal contributions (goals + assists, every competition), clean sheets, and each POTM award use the player’s playstyle. A player with no playstyle is treated as a Goal Poacher (forward), Box-to-Box (midfielder), Build Up (defender), or any goalkeeper. The whole total, including trophies and appearances, is then multiplied by the club’s Super League points ÷ 100. 54 league points is a 0.54 multiplier. 114 is 1.14.
