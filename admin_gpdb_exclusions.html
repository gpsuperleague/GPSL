GPDB season exclusions

← Admin hub · Season Break tool to remove players or nations from GPSL for a season (e.g. misconduct, conflict zones). Excluded players are hidden from GPDB and cannot be signed, drafted, or called up. Excluded nations cannot be claimed — their players, managers (MGDB / manager market), and clubs (Club Database) are treated as excluded too. Lists are per season — creating a new season auto-copies the previous season’s exclusions. You can also copy manually below. Requires patches/gpdb_season_exclusions.sql, patches/gpdb_season_exclusions_persist.sql, and patches/gpdb_season_exclusions_managers_clubs_20260815.sql.
