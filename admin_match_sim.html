Simulation rules

Card targets match the month-deploy discipline defaults (15 yellow / 1 red per GPSL calendar month). Remaining fixtures in the month share whatever quota is left.

How it works

  • Uses each club’s saved matchday starting XI (else top 11 by Rating).
  • XI Rating sum picks a band; that band rolls home / draw / away.
  • Star players nudge goal totals (~10%), not the win/draw/loss roll.
  • Assists + POTM always generated; cards/injuries follow the settings above.
