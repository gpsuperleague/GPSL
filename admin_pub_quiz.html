Pub quiz

Five rounds, ten questions each. Text rounds are 5 seconds. Picture and song rounds are 8 seconds. Prizes: ₿5,000 / ₿3,500 / ₿2,000, and ₿1,000 for everyone else who answers at least once. The quiz posts in the Discord channel named pub quiz. Starting one clears that channel first. Test in Discord posts three questions. It uses saved ones when a text question, a picture, and a song are already saved. Otherwise it posts a sample question, a yellow card, and a short tone. The 50 boxes stay empty until you press Save question.
