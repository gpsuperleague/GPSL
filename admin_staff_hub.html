Column key

Visits
One count per hour, including a remembered login.
U prev / U cur
Unplayed league and cup fixtures still live in that GPSL month.
U missed
Frozen each month, and it stays after leftovers are simmed. Press Record unplayed before you sim.
H
Next month’s fixtures, proposed/total. Only a proposal the home club sent. League homes are proposed in the month before; after the lock, away may open one if nothing is pending, and that is not counted. Either club can open a cup once the round month opens; an away opening is not counted. Vacant clubs are left out.
V late
Match video uploaded after that fixture’s month lock.
V fail
Still missing 72 hours after that lock. ₿500,000.
F tot
Season fines already applied.

F lock is what would be charged if the next month locked now. Auto fines are off, so this is only the projection.

  • No proposal from either club: ₿500,000 on the home club. Next month’s homes, plus leftovers still unproposed.
  • A proposal in the last 48 hours is not an extra fee.
  • A reply still overdue at the lock, after the 48-hour strike: another ₿500,000 on the club who owes the reply.
  • A third strike on the same fixture forfeits it and adds ₿1,000,000. That extra is not in this figure.
  • Left out: holiday, a manual black box, and a new owner’s first partial month. A mid-month appointment stays clear until their first full GPSL month, and unplayed league fixtures stay in the catch-up window through the join month plus three more.

Club auction — owners without a club

Owners invited to the club auction or on the waiting list, checked against the five required entry items on their Awaiting club page: Owner tag, Timezone, Match availability, Primary club interest and Backup club. Invited owners are listed first. Bidding shows the club each owner is currently the highest bidder on, whether they've been outbid, or that they haven't bid yet.

Manager auction — owned clubs

Every club with an owner, whether it has a manager, and its bidding in the current manager draft auction: the managers it's leading (with the current bid), outbid, or not bid yet. Owners may only lead one manager auction at a time — clubs leading 2+ are flagged and listed first. Owners still waiting for a club can't bid on managers until their club is assigned.
