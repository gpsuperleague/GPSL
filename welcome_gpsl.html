Welcome to GPSL

Global Pro Super League — a Discord-based football management league. Here’s how to get started without messaging admins for the basics.

1. Ways in

  1. Just browsing? Sign in as a Visitor with Discord — tables, cups, auctions and the transfer market (read only).
  2. Want a club (draft / pre-season)? Apply to join the league — creates your GPSL account and puts you on the waiting list (by Discord join date). When a slot opens, admin invites you to the club draft auction.
  3. Want a club mid-season? Apply the same way. On the waiting list you’ll see vacant clubs — pick one, then go straight into handpick (build from scratch and submit for approval). No auction bid.

Discord server members only. Paths b and c both start with Apply — what happens next depends on whether the league is in draft/pre-season or mid-season.

2. After you apply

  • You’ll land on the waiting list.
  • Fill in owner details (tag, timezone, availability).
  • Read the handbook — especially The Waiting Room.
  • Draft / pre-season: when invited, bid in the club draft auction.
  • Mid-season: pick a free club on the waiting list → handpick squad → submit for approval.

No need to DM for a place on the list — the apply button does that.

3. Discord roles

  • Visitor — in the server, no GPSL owner account yet.
  • Member — GPSL account (waiting list / on break / etc.).
  • Owner — has an assigned club.
