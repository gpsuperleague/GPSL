Welcome to GPSL

Global Pro Super League — a Discord football management league on eFootball. You do not need to message admins for the basics.

Checking whether GPSL is mid-season or in pre-season…

1. Browse the site (optional)

Sign in as a Visitor with Discord to follow tables, cups, auctions and the transfer market — read only. No club required.

Sign in as Visitor

2. Want a club?

Apply to join GPSL — creates your account and puts you on the waiting list (order by Discord join date). No DM to admin needed.

Apply to join the league

What happens next (mid-season)

  1. Open the waiting list.
  2. If a club is vacant, claim it.
  3. Build your starting squad (handpick) and submit for approval — no auction.
  4. Fill in your owner profile (Discord tag, timezone, when you can play).

What happens next (pre-season)

  1. Open the waiting list.
  2. Fill in your owner profile (Discord tag, timezone, when you can play).
  3. When a slot opens, admin invites you to the club draft auction — you bid for a club there.

What happens next depends on the calendar:

  • Mid-season (league already running): claim a vacant club on the waiting list → handpick → submit for approval.
  • Pre-season / setup: stay on the list, complete your owner profile, then join the club draft auction when invited.

3. Discord roles

  • Visitor — in the server, no GPSL account yet.
  • Member — GPSL account, no club yet (waiting list).
  • Owner — has an assigned club.

4. Learning the rules

Nobody expects you to know every rule on day one. After a few matches we do expect the basics (scheduling, check-in, results, transfers).

Use the league handbook, ask in Discord, and learn as you play.
