Become an owner

Five steps. Each one unlocks the next. A club stays closed until the video is approved. During the season you then pick an empty club. Between seasons you join the next auction.

Qualifying friendlies Post each friendly here. Both of you send the same line: your tag, the score, their tag. Example: YOURTAG 2-1 THEIRTAG. The other owner's matching post is what registers it. Five within 7 days. Qualifying videos Post the film of friendly 5 here. Your recording code goes in square brackets, then the YouTube or MEGA address in round brackets. Use the Discord account saved on your profile. Q&A Questions about qualifying go here, not in the friendlies or videos channels. Start a new thread. Write the question in the first message and tag staff, so the people who answer see it. Beginner's guide The short version of joining, handpick, money, and match day. Read it before you take a club.
