ℹ️ How to use it in Discord
📖 Read a message in your language Right-click the message (on a phone: press and hold) → Apps → Translate. You get a private translation — only you can see it.

🌍 Change language in Discord Type /language and choose from the list — same setting as this box.

The channels never change — nobody else sees your translations. If /english doesn't appear, restart Discord.

GPSL match system

ON (default) — use the normal GPSL propose / reply / Discord-lock flow, with arrangement deadlines and fines. OFF — leave the safety net: you arrange entirely off-site with any opponent who is also OFF (black box until the match is played). If that fixture is still unplayed when its GPSL month locks: mutual forfeit (0 points each — not a draw) and ₿2.5m each. Cups: both clubs go out; the next-round opponent gets a bye. If either club has GPSL ON, the normal GPSL system applies.

Windows: set Aug–Dec preference in July; set Jan–end preference in December (takes effect from January). No December change keeps your Aug–Dec choice.

GPSL arranging Propose / reply deadlines and arrangement fines apply.

Season absence

If you must miss a season, your club stays yours: league and cup fixtures are awarded (opponents get the results; relegation still applies), player contracts tick, your manager is released for market value into the club balance, and club money / loans freeze (nothing in or out — no wages, TV, prizes, challenges, friendlies cash). No personal wealth from this club that season. Market locked except FFP force-sell if you were already ≤ −₿100m. Rock-bottom forced transfer request still applies. Limit: 1 season in 4. You can return early to unfreeze remaining fixtures.

Holiday booking

Book real-world dates (up to 14 days per season, or 21 days while you are an active GPSL supporter). Extra days beyond 14 only stay valid while subscribed — if support lapses, the earliest bookings fill the 14-day budget and the rest pause (UH/OH, early unlock, fine relief) until you renew. Early arrange/play needs 4+ days overlapping a future GPSL month. Both clubs need a full squad (min 24). Cancel/amend only while upcoming.
