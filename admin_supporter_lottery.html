Supporters' lottery

On the 1st of each month (UK time) one current Ko-fi supporter is drawn at random, then a prize is drawn at random using the weights below. Club prizes go to the winner's club (Rewards Centre); a winner without a club gets the owner credits. The winner gets an inbox message and it's announced in #gpsl-news. Supporters are flagged on Owners.
