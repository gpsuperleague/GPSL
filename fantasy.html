GP Fantasy League

Optional side-game. Pick a 15-man squad from Super League & Championship contracted players. Prices are GPSL market values rounded to the nearest million (GPFL budget only — never club money).

GPFL is financially separate from GPSL club finances, Bookies, and the Central Bank. Points stay if a player becomes a free agent; you get a GPFL budget refund and must replace them.
