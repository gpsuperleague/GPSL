Nation selection

Owners pick a national team in rank order (rolling points from the last four seasons). Initial selection opens after Season 1; re-selection after each World Cup. Your nation is locked until the next World Cup cycle. When you claim, you also appoint a national manager (no fee, cannot be used by another nation). National managers are separate from club managers and leave after the next World Cup finals. Assigned nations link through to that nation’s squad page. Nations greyed out on the grid cannot be selected — the GPDB player pool is too small for a 23-man squad or a GPSL club.
