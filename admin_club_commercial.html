Commercial income

Bands & rules

Three Central Bank–funded streams per club per season: main sponsor, pitchside advertising (5 boards) and merchandising (club shop + global kit sales). Club tier comes from prestige (Stadium settings cut-offs). Results vs league & cup targets place a club inside its band.

Run

Automatic from GPSL June (checked every 10 minutes): pitchside boards are sold (priced on last season), existing sponsor deals are paid, and every owner gets 3 sponsor offers in their inbox. Vacant clubs get no offers — whoever takes the club later in the season gets them then (once per club per season). Owners who miss the deadline get the long-term deal. The button below just runs the same check now.

End of season (merchandising + performance-deal bonuses) runs automatically inside Close Finances. Use the button only if you need it earlier. Both are safe to re-run — nothing is paid twice.
