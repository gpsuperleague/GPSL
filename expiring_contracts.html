Expiring Contracts

Hidden wage bids — players in their final contract year (standard players only; home-grown ≤23 use Squad renew/expire). The player stays at the holding club all season while bids are collected. One wage offer per club; must be higher than current wage and in ₿250,000 steps. Competing amounts stay hidden. At season rollover, the highest bid wins a new 3-season contract. Ties favour the holding club. Another club winning pays market value to the holder; Championship clubs signing a Super League player also pay a ₿10m signing-on fee to the Central Bank.
