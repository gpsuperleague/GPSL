# Announcement — Get ready for August (season checklist)

**Suggested Natter / Discord title:** August is coming — finish your ready checklist

---

Owners,

A lot of clubs still have **open items** on the **season ready / August checklist**.

That checklist is not optional busywork. It’s the stuff that stops August kicking off with empty availability, no manager, unfinished squads, and home fixtures with no kick-off proposed.

### Where to look
Open your **Dashboard** and work through the ready checklist until it’s clear.

### What still needs doing (tick what’s left for you)
**Squad**
- 24–28 players
- At least 8 homegrown
- At least 1 GK and 5 U21s
- Stars inside your cap

**Club set-up**
- Manager signed
- Discord tag linked
- Match availability set (Owner Details — the weekly times you can play)

**Fixtures**
- Propose kick-offs for your **August & September home** matches (including cup ties)
- Reply to every proposal sitting in your **Inbox**

**Matchday ready** (self-ticks — open Learning GPSL if you need a refresh)
- You’ve read the Matchday checklist
- You can record and upload
- You know match settings, squad/tactics rules, kits & stadium rules

### Why this matters
If you’re not ready, your opponents can’t lock times cleanly, and August will start with avoidable mess: late proposals, fines pressure, and matches that shouldn’t still be floating.

Please finish what’s outstanding **now** — don’t wait for the month to unlock and hope it sorts itself out.

Dashboard → ready checklist. Owner Details for availability. Inbox for replies. Schedule pages for home proposals.

— GPSL
