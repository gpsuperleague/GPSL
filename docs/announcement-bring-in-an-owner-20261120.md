# Announcement — Bring in an owner

**Suggested Discord title:** Bring in an owner — ₿20,000

Not posted. Copy below is the Discord message.

---

**Discord post:**

```
Owners — bring someone in.

If you bring in a new owner, they take a club, and they are still a club owner at the end of a full GPSL season, you receive ₿20,000 owner credits. That is your personal wallet, not the club balance. Paid once, at the end of that season.

Name them when they join. The link is stored then. It cannot be added afterwards. If they never take a club, or they leave before that season ends, there is no payment.

A season you join part-way through does not count. The next full season does. Someone who already owned a club does not count. One new owner, one person who brought them in.

You can see who you brought in, and whether they are still in, from your owner page.
```

## Tracker

Recorded when the new owner is named, not at the end of the season.

| Field | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Brought in by | The current owner who named them |
| New owner | The person who joined |
| Club taken | Club and the date they took it. Blank until they do |
| Season that counts | The next full season if they joined mid-season, otherwise the season they start |
| Status | Named · Has a club · Still in · Left early · Paid |
| Paid | ₿20,000 into the bringer’s personal wallet, once, when that season ends and they are still a club owner |

The bringer sees their own list. Staff see every row. A name cannot be attached after the new owner already has a club.
