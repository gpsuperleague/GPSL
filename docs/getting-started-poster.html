01 · The club

On the waiting list you choose a free club. You keep the cash. The old squad is released. You Handpick the new one.

02 · The game

Open eFootball before you tag, bid, or sign. If the player is not in the game, leave them.

03 · The squad

24 to 28 players. 8 home-grown. 5 aged 21 or under. A goalkeeper. Stars from 79 up: 3 in the Super League, 2 in the Championship.

04 · The clock

August to December, then January to May, then playoffs. The month changes on Thursday at 7pm UK.

05 · The match

Home proposes the kick-off. The other club has 48 hours. Check in from 10 minutes before until 10 minutes after.

06 · The tape

Record it. 10 minutes, injuries off, PA3 or PA4. Post the video within 72 hours of the lock. Miss it and the fine is 500,000, plus a point.
