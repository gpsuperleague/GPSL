## Quick map

1. **Before KO** — availability → home proposes (30‑min mutual) or poke → away accept/counter → optional Discord lock
2. **Agreed** — check-in ±10 · play 30‑min block · Play now (earlier 10‑min) · mutual new time · drop
3. **Problems** — one-sided no-show ladder · both miss fine · catch-up reset
4. **Month lock** — no proposal ₿2.5m · late ₿1m · reply warn→₿2.5m · holiday corridor · both system OFF = mutual forfeit

## Holiday corridor — early arrange / early play

Only fixtures whose **GPSL play month** is hit by a qualifying holiday get this path. Other months stay normal (play in-month).

### When it applies

- Club books a holiday (up to **14** days/season, or **21** while an active GPSL supporter — days past 14 only stay valid while subscribed).
- Holiday must be booked **before that GPSL month opens**.
- **Qualifying hit:** holiday overlaps the fixture’s play-month unlock→lock by **4+ inclusive days** for home **or** away.
- League and cup only; fixture must still be `scheduled`.

### What changes for that fixture

| Phase | Unlock | Lock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before play month | Earliest calendar unlock at/before the play month → **arrange & play early** | Play month + grace, capped at season end |
| In play month / catch-up | Active GPSL month unlock | Same lock |

**Play-grace after the fixture month**

- League → **+2** GPSL months
- Cup → **+1**
- May cups → **+0** (season end)

### Early-play rules

- Both clubs need a **full squad (min 24)** to play early.
- Result can be **saved early**; league table / season stats wait until that GPSL month is active.
- Kick-off UI shows holiday note (“arrange/play early where possible” / fines paused when already away).
- **Home no-proposal fine (₿2.5m)** is suppressed while home is **on** a booked holiday; still expect arrange/play early where possible before / around the trip.

### What does **not** change

- Non-hit months stay normal: propose in the month before, play in the play month.
- Play now / poke / check-in / no-show ladders still apply once a KO is agreed.
- Catch-up after play-month close still uses the corridor lock (grace above), not unlimited early unlock into the past.
