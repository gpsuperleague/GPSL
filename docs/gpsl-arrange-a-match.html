How to arrange a match

Plain-English guide for owners. Pick the pathway that fits how you and your opponent talk — then lock the kick-off on the site before you play.

Golden rule (default): chatting on Discord is fine. Playing only counts after both clubs have an agreed kick-off on the GPSL schedule page, then both check in (±10 minutes of kick-off) and enter the result on Match Day. Exception: if both clubs have GPSL match system OFF, see Pathway 6 (manual black box).
Pathways in this guide
  1. Normal site propose / accept / counter
  2. Agreed on Discord → lock on the site
  3. Already locked — change time (mutual override)

Before any pathway

  • Set your weekly availability and timezone on Owner Details.
  • You need a signed manager before you can arrange or play.
  • Open the fixture from Fixtures or My club fixtures → Schedule match.
  • GPSL months run Thursday 19:00 UK → next Thursday 19:00 UK. Fines are usually charged when a month locks, not the second you go overdue.
Pathway 1

Normal site propose / accept / counter

Use this when you arrange everything on the website (Inbox + Schedule). Most common default.

  1. Home proposes first. Home opens Schedule, picks a 30‑minute slot from their availability, and presses Propose. Away does not need their availability set yet.
  2. Away gets an Inbox message. Accept the time, or counter with another slot.
  3. Keep going until someone accepts. Each new proposal is the other club’s turn. The site shows “your response due by…”.
  4. Kick-off is agreed. Both clubs see the locked time. Save Match Day squads, check in at kick-off, play.

Deadlines (reply clocks)

  • First proposal early (well before the play month opens) → away must reply by the play month open (Thursday 19:00 UK).
  • First proposal in the last 48 hours before the play month opens → 48 hours to reply.
  • First proposal after the play month has started → 48 hours.
  • Any counter / later offer → 24 hours.
  • Catch-up → always 24 hours (see pathway 4).

Fines (home arrangement)

  • Home should propose in the month before the fixture’s play month (e.g. September game → propose before August locks).
  • No home proposal by that lock → ₿2.5m (can repeat each later lock until they propose).
  • Home first proposes only in the last 48 hours before the play month opens → ₿1m late fee (not stacked with the no-proposal fine at the same check).

Fines (missed reply)

  • Still your turn and overdue when a GPSL month locks → first time: Inbox warning only.
  • Still overdue at a later month lock → ₿2.5m (can repeat). Negotiation is not wiped — reply whenever you can.
Pathway 2

Agreed on Discord → lock on the site

Use this when you already shook hands in Discord (or chat). You still must both confirm the same kick-off on GPSL.

  1. Talk on Discord and pick a kick-off time (use club timezones / UK labels as on the schedule page).
  2. Either club opens Schedule while the fixture is still unscheduled / negotiating.
  3. Use “We agreed on Discord — lock this kick-off”. Pick the slot you agreed and send it. Opponent gets Inbox / Schedule confirm.
  4. Opponent confirms. Kick-off becomes agreed. (This first lock does not use your one mutual change later.)
Clocks keep running. Discord lock does not pause reply or arrangement deadlines. Until both of you lock the time on the site, home can still get arrangement fines and whoever owes a reply can still get the warning / reply fine path.

Deadlines

  • Same reply / arrangement deadlines as Pathway 1 until the kick-off is locked.
  • Confirm request expires in 24 hours if the opponent never confirms — send again if needed.

Fines

  • Same as Pathway 1 if you leave it unlocked past deadlines.
  • No extra fine for using Discord lock itself.
Pathway 3

Already locked — change time (mutual override)

Use this when kick-off is already agreed and both clubs want a new time (or “play now”) without burning your monthly reschedule or an emergency drop.

  1. Open Schedule on the agreed fixture.
  2. Choose Play now (if offered) or pick a new mutual slot and request the change.
  3. Opponent confirms in Inbox / Schedule within 24 hours.
  4. One mutual change per fixture. After that, use voluntary / emergency drop (if allowed) or play as booked.

Deadlines

  • Confirm within 24 hours or the request expires.
  • Does not pause anything else once applied — new kick-off becomes the agreed time.

Fines / allowances

  • No fine and no use of your monthly reschedule or emergency drop when both agree.
  • If you need to move it alone instead, see drops under After you lock.
Pathway 4

Catch-up (play month already finished)

Use this when the fixture’s play month ended and the match is still unplayed. Fixtures shows a catch-up badge. You can still arrange and play in a later GPSL month (within the play window).

  1. Open the catch-up fixture on Schedule.
  2. Arrange like Pathway 1 or 2 — propose / counter on the site, or Discord-lock the time you agreed.
  3. Propose times in the current GPSL month’s slots. Replies are always due within 24 hours.
  4. If an old agreed time went stale, use the schedule page reset (“pick new time” / catch-up reset) — that does not use your monthly reschedule.

Deadlines / window

  • Every reply: 24 hours.
  • League play window: scheduled month + 2 (e.g. September → through November).
  • Cups: scheduled month + 1 (May cups: no extra month).
  • After the window, still unplayed → awarded by activity (or 0–0 if tied).

Fines

  • Missed reply: same warn first, then ₿2.5m path at month locks.
  • Recorded no-show still unresolved at a lock → 3–0 + ₿2.5m.
  • Voluntary / emergency drops are not available on catch-up.
Pathway 5

Holiday early play

If a booked owner holiday overlaps a later GPSL month, some fixtures unlock early so you can arrange and play ahead. Both clubs need a full squad (min 24).

  1. Book holiday on Owner Details before that GPSL month opens (limits apply).
  2. When the fixture shows as holiday early, arrange with Pathway 1 or 2 as usual.
  3. Play in the early window shown on the schedule page.

Deadlines

  • Same propose / reply clocks as normal once unlocked.
  • Arrangement / reply / no-proposal fines can be suppressed while home is on a booked holiday for that month — details on Owner Details / month-end guide.

Fines

  • Once you are arranging, missed replies and no-shows follow the same rules as other pathways when they apply.
  • Leaving the holiday month without playing can forfeit at award time — see the month-end guide.
Pathway 6

GPSL match system OFF (manual black box)

Opt-out of the safety net. Default is ON. Toggle on Owner Details. Only applies when both clubs are OFF for that half-season.

  1. Set preference in the right window. July → Aug–Dec. December → Jan onward (activates from January). Missing preference = ON.
  2. Both OFF → Fixtures shows “Manual arrange”. No propose / reply deadlines. Arrange off-site. Enter the result on Match Day during the GPSL month.
  3. Still unplayed at that month’s lock → mutual forfeit (0 pts each, not a draw) + ₿2.5m each. Cups: both go out; next-round opponent gets a bye.

Who uses which path?

  • Either club ON → Pathways 1–5 as normal.
  • Both OFF → this pathway only.

Fines

  • No arrangement / late-proposal fines while both OFF.
  • Unplayed at lock → ₿2.5m each + mutual forfeit.

After you lock: check-in, drops, no-shows

Same for Pathways 1–5 once kick-off is agreed. Pathway 6 has no site kick-off.

  1. Save a valid Match Day squad before kick-off (11 starters incl. GK, 12 subs, no injured/suspended). Check-in is refused otherwise.
  2. Both check in on the schedule page — opens 10 minutes before kick-off, closes 10 minutes after.
  3. Both checked in → Match Day unlocks for about 30 minutes to enter the result.

If check-in goes wrong

  • Both miss → no fine. Pick a new time (replay / catch-up reset) and try again.
  • Only one checks in → no-show recorded; both clubs get an Inbox warning. You can still play and confirm a normal result to clear it.
  • If a month lock arrives and that no-show is still unresolved → no-show club gets 3–0 + ₿2.5m.

Moving an agreed kick-off alone

  • Voluntary drop (≥24h before KO) — 1 per GPSL month; back to arranging. Not on catch-up.
  • Emergency drop (<24h before KO) — 2 per season. If none left → 3–0 + ₿3m. Not on catch-up.
  • Or use Pathway 3 (mutual) if both agree — no allowance used.

Quick questions

Do I have to use Discord?

No. Pathway 1 is fully on the site. Discord is optional — just lock the time on Schedule if you agree there.

We agreed on Discord but forgot to lock — can we be fined?

Yes. Until both confirm on the site, arrangement and reply clocks still run. Lock as soon as you agree.

I got a reply warning — do I owe money now?

Not for that first lock. Reply as soon as you can. If you are still overdue at a later month lock, then ₿2.5m can apply.

Does this apply to cups?

Yes — same arrange / check-in / fine rules for league and cups (play window length differs for cups).
