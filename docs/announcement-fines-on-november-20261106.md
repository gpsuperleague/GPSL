## The ones that catch people

| Charge | What it is | How to avoid it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **₿2.5m** | League home never proposed by the lock **before** the play month. Repeats every later lock until they propose. | Propose in the month before. Holiday covering that month suppresses it — still arrange around the trip. |
| **₿2.5m** | Cup still has no home proposal when the **grace month** after the round locks. | Cups are drawn when the round month opens. Arrange **in that month**. The **next month is grace** (May cups: no extra month). You are not fined for the month before the tie existed. |
| **₿1m** | League only. Home’s first proposal was in the **last 48 hours** before the play month opened. | Propose before that 48-hour window. Cups do not pay this. |
| **Warning, then ₿1m** | A scheduling reply was still overdue at month lock. | Reply on Schedule / Inbox. The first overdue lock is a warning. A later lock, still overdue, is the fine. Negotiation is not wiped. |
| **₿500k / ₿1m / ₿1.5m** | 1st / 2nd / 3rd no-show on that fixture, plus a season strike each time. | Check in from **10 minutes before** kick-off until **10 minutes after**. |
| **0–3** | 3rd no-show on the same fixture. | Same check-in window. Do not collect three misses. |
| **₿2.5m** | Every 3rd season no-show strike (3, 6, 9…). | Keep the strike count down. Check in. |
| **₿500k and −1 point** | Your match video is still missing after the play month locks **plus 72 hours**. The point is suspended for 24 hours first. | Upload your own video in that window. Upload inside the 24 hours and the point is cancelled. Still missing after that: **−1**, and it counts as a full strike. |
| **−9 now, −3 next season** | 3rd full missing-video strike in the season. | Do not collect three missed videos. |
| **Replay, or the result stands, plus −1 at 2+ drags** | Players dragged off the default formation. Staff approve a video report before anything changes. | Set Subtactic and Fluid on a **default Konami formation before** the eFootball match. **No dragging.** Offender won or drew → replay. Opponent won → result stands. **2 or more drags** in that match → **−1 league point** as well. |

## Matchday recording — reported on the video

An opponent uses the **R** button on the uploaded video. Nothing is charged until staff approve. Each line is one reportable breach. The rule next to it is how you stay off the report.

| Fine | Avoid it by |
| --- | --- |
| **₿20m — DOGSO** | Do not deny an obvious goalscoring opportunity. |
| **₿10m — Deliberately scoring an own goal** | Play the match. Do not put the ball in your own net on purpose. |
| **₿10m — Deliberately attempt to score an own goal** | Do not try it either. |
| **₿10m — Deliberately engineer opposition chances** | Do not tee the opponent up. |
| **₿10m — Refusing to participate** | Play the agreed match. |
| **₿10m — Time wasting** | Keep the game moving. |
| **₿10m — Showboating** | Play the match, do not perform. |
| **₿10m — Playing whilst the opponent is incapacitated** | Stop and wait if they disconnect or cannot play. |
| **₿10m — Form manipulation** | Do not tilt the match to shape a result. |
| **₿10m — Deliberate foul play** is **₿7.5m** | No cynical or staged fouls. |
| **₿10m — Illegal match termination** | Finish the match. Do not quit out. |
| **₿10m — Non display PA level** | Show the PA level on the recording. |
| **₿10m — Use of PA1, PA2 or SA** | Do not use PA1, PA2, or Smart Assist. |
| **₿10m — Non display attack/defence level** | Show attack and defence levels. |
| **₿5m — Non display scoreboard** | Leave the scoreboard on the recording. |
| **₿10m — Non display sub tactic** | Show the sub tactic. It must be a default formation. |
| **₿10m — Individual player instructions used** | Instructions blank. |
| **₿10m — Red/Blue before the 80th minute** | Single Red or Blue only from the 80th minute. |
| **₿10m — Double Red/Blue before the 90th minute** | Double Red or Blue only from the 90th. |
| **₿5m — No GK** | A goalkeeper in the starting XI. |
| **₿10m — Matchroom settings not displayed (home)** | Home: show the matchroom settings on the video. |
| **₿2.5m — Matchroom settings not cross-checked and displayed (away)** | Away: cross-check the settings and show them. |
| **₿2.5m — Incorrect stadium** | The stadium GPSL expects for that match. |
| **₿2.5m — Incorrect season** | The correct eFootball season. |
| **₿10m — Incorrect pitch conditions** | The pitch condition set for the fixture. |
| **₿10m — Incorrect weather** | The weather set for the fixture. |
| **₿2.5m — Incorrect club selection** | Your club, not a stand-in. |
| **₿2.5m — Incorrect kit** | The right kit. |
| **₿1m — Incorrect club name and abbreviation** | Name and short name as registered. |
| **₿1m — Incorrect custom stadium settings** | Custom stadium matches the fixture settings. |
| **₿10m — Ineligible manager** | The manager signed to your club. |
| **₿2.5m — Ineligible player, unused sub** | Do not name an ineligible player even on the bench unused. |
| **₿5m — Ineligible starter, off by 10 minutes, no score** | Do not start an ineligible player. |
| **₿10m — Ineligible starter who plays more than 10 minutes** | Same rule. Longer they play, larger the fine. |
| **₿10m — Ineligible player introduced as a sub** | Do not bring an ineligible player on. |
| **Formation drag** | Default Konami formation set before the match. No dragging. Outcome is replay or the result stands, and **−1 point** if there were 2 or more drags. Staff confirm the report first. |

Pitch behaviour on Match Day still stands: no DOGSO, no timewasting, no match manipulation.

## Also still real

- **Both clubs GPSL match system OFF**, fixture still unplayed at month lock → **mutual forfeit** (0 points each) and **₿2.5m each**. Cups: both go out.
- **Emergency drop** with no season allowance left (2 per season) → **₿3m and 3–0**.
- **Board expectation miss** can take **10%** of your personal Building Society balance. That is separate from these match fines.

New owners appointed mid-month still have the scheduling-fine grace already on the site. It does not cover a no-show, a missing video, or a recording breach.

If a number on your ledger ever disagrees with this post, the ledger is the one that was applied. Learning GPSL → Discipline has the same rules in long form.
