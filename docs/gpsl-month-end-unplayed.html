Month end & unplayed fixtures

A plain-English guide for club owners. How GPSL months work, what happens if a league match isn’t played in time, and which fines can apply.

The big myth: if you don’t play a league game in its month, it is not automatically a 3–0 forfeit. It usually becomes a catch-up game you can still arrange and play later. Fines for not proposing / not replying can still land — even if the match itself survives.

In one minute

  1. One real week = one GPSL month
    Each GPSL month runs Friday 19:00 UK → next Friday 19:00 UK (August through May, then Playoffs). The badge in the nav shows which month is live.
  2. Home proposes first
    Agree a kick-off on the site (Fixtures → Schedule, or Inbox). Set your weekly availability on Owner Details first. Discord is optional for chatting — deadlines are on the site.
  3. Arrange early, play in the fixture’s month
    Example: a September fixture should be arranged before August ends, then played during September.
  4. Missed the play month?
    League games still unplayed become catch-up (highlighted on Fixtures). Keep arranging and playing them in later months.

What happens when a GPSL month ends?

At Friday 19:00 UK (or if an admin ends the month early), the league checks scheduling for that month. This is about league fixtures.

How do we agree a time?

  • Home club proposes first — pick a slot from your availability.
  • Away gets an Inbox message — Accept, or counter with their own slot.
  • After a couple of proposals each way, the site may suggest sorting it on Discord, then picking a slot on the schedule page.
  • You must reply within the deadlines below. Missed replies are tracked during the week; the fine is charged when that fixture’s play month ends, not the moment you go overdue.

Match day: check-in & play

  • At the agreed kick-off, both owners open the schedule page and Check in within 10 minutes.
  • Once both have checked in, you have a 30-minute window to enter the result on Match Day.
  • Both miss check-in: no fine. Use “pick a new time” on the schedule page and try again.
  • Only one checks in: the site records a no-show but does not forfeit instantly. If you still play and confirm a normal result later, there is no no-show fine. If the play month ends and you never finished the match, the no-show club gets 3–0 and ₿5m.

Need to move the kick-off?

  • Voluntary drop (24 hours or more before kick-off) — uses your one reschedule per GPSL month. Back to arranging; no forfeit. Not available on catch-up games.
  • Emergency drop (less than 24 hours before kick-off) — uses one of your two per season. If you’ve used both, you forfeit 3–0 and ₿3m. Not available on catch-up games.
  • Mutual override — either club can ask to play now or pick a new time; both must confirm in Inbox (request expires in 24 hours). One use per fixture; does not use your drop allowances.

Fines (league)

These are charged when the relevant GPSL month closes. You’ll see them in Inbox and on your balance.

Catch-up games

If a league fixture’s play month has finished and the match is still not played, Fixtures marks it as catch-up. Propose times in the current GPSL month. Replies are always due within 24 hours. You can’t use voluntary/emergency drops on catch-up fixtures — reset a stale agreed time on the schedule page instead (that does not use your monthly reschedule).

Common questions

Do I have to message on Discord within X hours?

No separate Discord clock. Contact and deadlines are on the site: propose, accept, or counter via Fixtures / Schedule / Inbox. Discord is fine for arranging informally — just put the agreed slot on the site.

We both forgot check-in — do we lose 3–0?

No. Pick a new time on the schedule page and try again. No fine for both missing.

I checked in, they didn’t — is it a forfeit now?

Not immediately. If you later play and confirm a normal result, you’re fine. Only if the play month ends with that no-show still unresolved do they get 3–0 and ₿5m.

We’re still negotiating when the month ends — is the game dead?

For league: usually no — it becomes catch-up. You might still get a missed-reply fine if a turn was overdue. Home can also get arrangement fines if they never proposed in time.

Does this apply to cup ties?

You still propose, check in, and play the same way. The month-end arrangement / reply / no-show fine jobs and catch-up flag described here are aimed at league fixtures.
