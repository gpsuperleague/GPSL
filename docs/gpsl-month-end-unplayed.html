During play month (quick)

  • Check-in: 10 minutes from KO · Play block: 30 minutes
  • Both miss check-in: no fine — pick new time
  • One-sided miss: recorded; normal result later clears with no fine
  • Voluntary drop ≥24h: 1/month · Emergency <24h: 2/season (else ₿3m+3–0) · both disabled on catch-up
  • Mutual override: both confirm, expires 24h, 1 per fixture
