Month end & unplayed fixtures

A plain-English guide for club owners. How GPSL months work, what happens if a league or cup match isn’t played in time, and which fines can apply. Same rules for all cups.

The big myth: if you don’t play a league or cup game in its month, it is not automatically a 3–0 forfeit. It usually becomes a catch-up game you can still arrange and play later. Fines for not proposing / not replying can still land — even if the match itself survives.

In one minute

  1. One real week = one GPSL month
    Each GPSL month runs Thursday 19:00 UK → next Thursday 19:00 UK (August through May, then Playoffs). The badge in the nav shows which month is live.
  2. Agree a kick-off
    League: home proposes in the month before the match. After that month locks, away can open it if nothing is waiting on a reply. Cups: either club can propose from when the round month opens. If nobody proposes, the strike stays on the home club. Set your weekly availability on Owner Details first. Discord is optional for chatting — deadlines are on the site. Step-by-step pathways: How to arrange a match.
  3. Arrange early, play in the fixture’s month
    Example: a September fixture should be arranged before August ends, then played during September.
  4. Missed the play month?
    League and cup games still unplayed become catch-up (highlighted on Fixtures). Keep arranging and playing them in later months.

What happens when a GPSL month ends?

At Thursday 19:00 UK (or if an admin ends the month early), the league checks scheduling for that month — for league and all cup fixtures.

How do we agree a time?

  • League: home proposes first, in the month before the match. After that month locks, away can open it if nothing is waiting on a reply.
  • Cups: either club can propose from when the round month opens. If nobody proposes, the strike stays on the home club.
  • The other club gets an Inbox message — Accept, or counter with their own slot.
  • You can agree informally on Discord anytime — then use We agreed on Discord — lock this kick-off on the schedule page so both clubs confirm on the site (reply clocks keep running until locked).
  • Every reply is 48 hours, and that clock can cross a month lock. Missing it is a strike: ₿500k. Still silent at the next lock is another. The proposal stays open.

Match day: check-in & play

  • At the agreed kick-off, both owners open the schedule page and check in from 10 minutes before until 10 minutes after.
  • Once both have checked in, you have a 30-minute window to play.
  • Only one checks in: a strike, ₿500k. The 3rd strike on that fixture forfeits it and adds ₿1m.
  • Both miss: each club takes a strike, ₿500k, and you pick a new time.
  • Season strikes: every strike is ₿500k. Every 5th (5, 10, 15…) adds another ₿1m.
  • Still unplayed at the end of the window: more strikes on that fixture loses, then a missed check-in, or a kick-off left sitting after the 48-hour reply, then scheduling activity. Level on all of that and the match is void.

Need to move the kick-off?

  • Voluntary drop (24 hours or more before kick-off) — uses your one reschedule per GPSL month. Back to arranging; no forfeit. Not available on catch-up games.
  • Emergency drop (less than 24 hours before kick-off) — uses one of your two per season. If you’ve used both, you forfeit 3–0 and ₿3m. Not available on catch-up games.
  • Mutual override — either club can ask to play now or pick a new time; both must confirm in Inbox (request expires in 24 hours). One free mutual new time per fixture; Play now can be used again if both agree. Does not use your drop allowances.

Catch-up games

If a league or cup fixture’s play month has finished and the match is still not played, Fixtures marks it as catch-up. Propose times in the current GPSL month. Replies are due within 48 hours. You can’t use voluntary/emergency drops on catch-up fixtures — reset a stale agreed time on the schedule page instead (that does not use your monthly reschedule).

Common questions

Do I have to message on Discord within X hours?

No separate Discord clock. Contact and deadlines are on the site: propose, accept, or counter via Fixtures / Schedule / Inbox. Discord is fine for arranging informally — then use We agreed on Discord — lock this kick-off on the schedule page so both confirm.

We both forgot check-in — do we lose 3–0?

No. Pick a new time on the schedule page and try again. No fine for both missing.

I checked in, they didn’t — is it a forfeit now?

Not immediately. If you later play and confirm a normal result, you’re fine. Only if a month lock arrives with that no-show still unresolved (play month or catch-up) do they get 3–0 and ₿2.5m.

We’re still negotiating when the month ends — is the game dead?

For league and cups: usually no — it becomes catch-up. You might still get a missed-reply fine if a turn was overdue. Home can also get arrangement fines if they never proposed in time.

Does this apply to cup ties?

Reply clocks, strikes, and check-in are the same. Who may open the tie is not. Either club can propose a cup from when the round month opens. The no-proposal strike stays on the home club, at that month’s lock. A cup is played in its round month and the month after (May cups: that month only). Champions League, FA Cup, and any other cup on the fixtures list are included.
