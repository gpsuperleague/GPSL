# Announcement — Fixed positions (default formations)

**Image:** `docs/announcement-fixed-positions-20261030.jpg`  
**Suggested Discord title:** Hands off the positions — default formations only (Fluid too)

---

Owners,

Quick reminder with love (and a tiny bit of side-eye):

**Player positions are fixed by the default formation.**  
Do **not** drag them around manually to invent your own DIY shape.

That rule applies to **Subtactic and Fluid alike**.  
Fluid On is fine — DIY rearranging in Fluid is not.

Your LB is not a secret false nine.  
Your DMF is not “basically a centre-forward if you squint.”  
Konami already did the seating plan — leave the furniture where it is.

### What is allowed
• **Default formations** only (Subtactic **and** Fluid)  
• **Fluid Formation** — On preferred, still on a **default** formation — **no manual moves**  
• **Player instructions** — blank  
• **GP manager** only  
• Show Manager · Subtactic · Squad on the recording as usual  
• GPSL Match Day squad: only the usual mirror role swaps (LB↔RB, LWF↔RWF, LMF↔RMF) where the site allows

### What is not allowed
• Dragging players around in eFootball to invent a custom shape — **including under Fluid**  
• Parking a full-back at CF “for vibes”  
• Individual player instructions  
• “Just this once” experimental furniture rearranging

Breach → reportable (see Learning GPSL → Discipline — display / instructions territory can hit the big fine).

Match Day checklist and Learning GPSL have the full console list. When in doubt: pick a default formation, leave positions alone, play the match.

Hands off the dots. Play with purpose.

— GPSL
