  1. 1

    Home goes first

    Nothing has changed here. The home club is the one who opens the match.

  2. 2

    There is a deadline for that first proposal

    League: the lock of the month before the match. Cups: the lock of the round month. No proposal from home by that lock counts as missed.

  3. 3

    Away can then name the kick-off

    On the Schedule page, the away club picks the time. That is the first proposal. It stops further no-proposal strikes.

  4. 4

    Home replies, as normal

    Accept it, or counter. The reply clock is the usual one. Away does not get a second opening while a time is already waiting.
