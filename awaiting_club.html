Club auction entry checklist

To enter the club draft auction you must complete every required item below — including marking a primary club and a backup club on the Club Database. Holidays are optional and do not block entry.

    Set your owner tag (shown on the waiting list and as the auction leader). Once you are invited to the club auction, the tag cannot be changed.

    Set your timezone and match availability — both are required for the club auction. Your timezone matters: it is how other owners see when you can play, so pick the city nearest you (or use Use my device timezone). Availability blocks are entered in UK time.

    Upcoming holidays (optional)

    Going away soon? Note it here so fixtures can be planned around you. Not required for the auction. When you are given a club, these move across to your club's holiday bookings automatically.
